BLOCKOS VILLE Parents

Safety Guide

Never share passwords, home addresses, school details, phone numbers or private photos. Use respectful language and leave conversations that feel unsafe.

Account Help

Use the email and password used at registration. Keep your password private and use the sign-out button on shared devices. Account recovery tools are not implemented yet.

Community Rules

No harassment, threats, impersonation, spam or personal information. Reporting and moderation controls are not implemented yet, so this first version should be used with supervision.

FAQs

Blockos Ville is an early prototype. The Catalog, avatar and forum require a configured Supabase project for shared persistent data. Do not make purchases with real money; Blockos Coins are prototype currency.

BLOCKOS VILLE is an independent game platform project.
